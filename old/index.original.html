Our Practice

Ruthlessly pragmatic

Callan House Rail provides independent expert counsel to the freight rail industry: product feedback, assessments, diligence, reviews, and retained advisory. Every engagement is built around a clear question and delivers a clear answer.

Rail technology decisions get made inside real constraints: a budget set last year, a crew that will use the system at three in the morning, a regulator with a date on the calendar, a vendor roadmap that may or may not arrive on time. Every recommendation here accounts for all of them, starts from what is actually running today, and lands somewhere you can defend to your board, your auditors, and the people who have to operate it.

The practice draws on a career spent on the operating side of rail technology: CIO of two railroad holding companies, a complete IT carve-out from a Fortune 500 parent, a Positive Train Control program delivered on statutory deadline, and merger integrations of core rail operating systems. For a supplier, that background puts the voice of the customer in the room. For an investor, it means diligence from someone who has run what you're evaluating. For a railroad, it means counsel from a peer who has signed the same purchase orders and answered to the same auditors.

Principal

J. Alex Lang

Alex Lang has led technology for the freight rail industry since 2014: as Chief Information Officer of Transtar, LLC and of Carload Express, Inc., and as solutions architect for the integration of Transtar and the Wheeling & Lake Erie. His portfolio spans the whole railroad: ERP and back office, communications and signal, dispatch, locomotive systems, cybersecurity, and regulatory compliance, all delivered within the lean budgets and union environments where shortline and regional railroads actually operate.

Before railroading, he spent twelve years in IT leadership at Carnegie Mellon University. He holds an MS in Information Technology Management from CMU's Heinz College. A lifelong student of the industry, he also co-owns The Station Inn, the storied railfan bed & breakfast trackside in Cresson, Pennsylvania.

Callan House Rail also conducts independent research and development in freight rail technology.
